Set up your own booking app

About 15 minutes, and you do it yourself. Your first 30 days are free; after that a once-off R150 registration fee and your monthly plan. Your clients' bookings, payments and photos are kept in a Google Sheet in your own Google account.

1Your business

Your clients' link will be …/?b=yourname

2Make your back office (Google Sheet)

You need a free Google (Gmail) account. Do this on a computer if you can – it's easier.

  1. Copy my back office – Google asks "Make a copy?" → tap Make a copy. The template link isn't ready yet – WhatsApp ISS and we'll send it.
  2. In your new sheet, wait a few seconds for the 🐾 Bookings menu at the top, then choose 🐾 Bookings → 1. Set up back office.
  3. Google asks for permission: tap Continue → choose your account. If it says "Google hasn't verified this app", tap Advanced → Go to … (unsafe) → Allow. (It's your own copy, running only in your account.) Then choose 1. Set up back office again.
  4. Open Extensions → Apps Script. At the top right tap Deploy → New deployment. Click the ⚙️ next to "Select type" → Web app.
  5. Set Execute as: Me and Who has access: Anyone → Deploy → allow access again if asked.
  6. Copy the Web app URL (it starts with https://script.google.com/macros/s/ and ends with /exec).

3Connect your back office to your app

4Make it yours

In your Google Sheet choose 🐾 Bookings → Business settings form and fill in:

  • Your look: business name, short line, main and accent colour, and upload your logo
  • Prices, hours and house rules, and most dogs at a time (this switches on your booking calendar)
  • Bank details for EFT, and your Yoco or SnapScan link if you take cards
  • Your app link (below) – so the photo links you send clients open your app
  • Your WhatsApp number and a free CallMeBot key if you want a WhatsApp message for every booking
On your phone: in the sheet choose 🐾 Bookings → 📱 My back office on my phone and send the link to your WhatsApp. From there you check payments, see who's here today and add daily photos of each dog.

5Share your app with your clients

Your clients book, pay, sign in and see their dog's photos here:

Open my app Share on WhatsApp Download my QR code

Clients install it from the link (Android: Install app; iPhone: Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen). They sign in under 📷 Photos with their WhatsApp number and a booking reference – no password.
